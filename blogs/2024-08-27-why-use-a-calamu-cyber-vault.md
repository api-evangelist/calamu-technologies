What is a cyber vault and why do you need one? When it comes to ransomware protection and overall cyber resilience, nothing is more critical than ensuring backup data is intact and available. However, this has become increasingly difficult in recent years as cyber attackers have started focusing on compromising the backup repositories first in an attack.
