What is Cybersecurity Awareness Month? October marks the 21st year of Cybersecurity Awareness Month , an international initiative dedicated to educating everyone—from young digital users to large multinational enterprises—about protecting their digital footprint. As technology becomes more intertwined with our everyday lives, the risks have grown as well, making cybersecurity more critical than ever.
