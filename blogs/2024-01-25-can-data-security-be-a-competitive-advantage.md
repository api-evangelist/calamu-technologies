Cybersecurity is not only essential for safeguarding a business and ensuring its longevity, it can also serve as a strategic approach to gain a competitive advantage. This is especially true for businesses offering B2B professional services, where winning large accounts often hinges on demonstrating a strong commitment to data security. Many organizations incorporate a third-party vendor cybersecurity audit into their selection process to verify compliance with the company's security standards and regulations.
