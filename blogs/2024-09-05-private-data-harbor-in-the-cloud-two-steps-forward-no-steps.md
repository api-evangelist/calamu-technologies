Technological trends can be a roller coaster, and cloud repatriation is a great example. Cloud repatriation is the process of moving workloads from public cloud resources back to on-premises or private cloud infrastructure, and is becoming a significant trend among organizations. According to a 2024 Barclays CIO Survey, 83% of companies are now planning or actively implementing cloud repatriation strategies, a sharp increase from just 43% in 2020.
